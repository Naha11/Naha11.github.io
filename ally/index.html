why now

Accessibility became law,
not a nice-to-have.

The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025. It covers any business serving customers in the EU, regardless of where that business is registered — a shop outside Europe taking European orders falls under it too. The operative standard is WCAG 2.1 level AA.

Enforcement bodies across all 27 member states are already handling complaints, issuing warnings and, in several countries, fines. Only microenterprises are exempt: fewer than ten staff and under two million euro turnover. Everyone above that line has to comply.

the difference

One color. Two verdicts.

Framer color styles store separate values for light and dark themes. A grey that reads perfectly on white quietly collapses on a dark background — same layer, same style, opposite verdict.

Every checker in the marketplace looks at one theme. Ally reports both, so a dark-mode failure never ships unnoticed.

What Ally does not do.

No automated tool can certify full WCAG compliance, and any tool claiming otherwise is selling you a false sense of safety. Ally covers the static, checkable part: contrast, alternative text, heading structure and target sizes. It does not test screen reader behaviour, keyboard navigation order, or text placed over images and gradients. Those still need a human, and Ally is built to make that human's job smaller — not to replace it.

Privacy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Ally is an accessibility audit plugin for Framer. This page says exactly what it sends, what it keeps, and what this website collects. It is short because there is little to say.

What the plugin sends

Ally makes one kind of network request, and only in the paid version: a licence check to api.lemonsqueezy.com. That request carries your licence key and an activation identifier. Nothing else leaves your machine.

Your pages are read and analysed entirely inside the Framer editor on your own computer. Layer names, text, colours, images and page structure are never transmitted anywhere. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no usage tracking and no advertising code in the plugin.

What the plugin stores

If you activate a licence, Ally stores your licence key, an activation identifier and the date of the last successful check. These live in Framer's own plugin storage, attached to your project. Removing the plugin or pressing Unlink removes them.

Audit results are held in memory while the panel is open and are gone when you close it.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. They collect the billing details and tax information required to sell to you, and they issue your licence key by email. Their privacy policy governs that data: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy. We never see or store your card details.

This website

These pages are static files served by GitHub Pages. They set no cookies, run no analytics and have no login.

Two things are worth stating plainly rather than hiding:

  • Fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Requesting a font reveals your IP address to Google, as it does on any site that loads fonts this way.
  • GitHub keeps standard server logs for pages it serves, which include IP addresses. That is GitHub's processing, not ours.

Your rights

We hold no account for you and no database of users. If you bought a licence, the personal data involved sits with Lemon Squeezy, and requests to see, correct or delete it are handled by them.

Contact

Questions about this page, or about anything Ally does with your data: lastakhanlast@gmail.com.

Changes

If the plugin ever starts sending something it does not send today, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
